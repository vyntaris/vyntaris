# Code of conduct

Vyntaris is made in the open, by people who give their time. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull
requests, reviews or anywhere the project is, does so under these few rules.

## What we do

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** Most problems are misunderstandings; ask before you judge.
- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Say what's wrong with a change and why, never who's wrong.
- **Welcome people who are new.** A first issue or pull request deserves patience and a clear answer.
- **Accept a no gracefully,** and give reasons when you say one.

## What we don't

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination of any kind, sexual language or attention, publishing
someone's private information, and keeping on with something after being asked to stop.

## When it happens

Write to vyntarisos@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept private, and answered within a
week. Depending on what happened, the answer can be a word, a warning, a removed comment, or a ban from
the project. Whoever reports in good faith is never at fault for it.

This applies in every space of the project, and when someone speaks for it elsewhere.
